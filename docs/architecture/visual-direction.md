# Signal to Story

Let transformation carry the explanation. Wide forms become measured signals, selected fragments, and narrow frames. Shape and sequence should communicate the idea before a reader reaches the captions. Every illustration is deliberately constructed rather than borrowed from an unrelated icon library.

Use warm paper, dark ink and a clear blue as the visual foundation. Coral marks a chosen fragment; muted green provides a calm setting for the footage. Precise alignment, consistent line weights and carefully balanced contrast give a small number of elements a professional finish.

Typography has two jobs: a characterful, bold heading establishes the subject, while a quiet regular face supplies short anchors. Never shrink a paragraph to make it fit. Remove implementation details from the overview and preserve them in the linked architecture guide.

The composition follows one vertical rhythm, with a label on the left and its visual explanation on the right. Equal spacing and restrained repetition make the sequence legible. Meticulous refinement should improve alignment, silhouette and hierarchy rather than introduce decorative complexity. The speakers, waveform and captions are illustrative, not screenshots or measured results.
